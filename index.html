Passport Photo Tool — runs 100% locally, nothing is uploaded anywhere

1 · Requirements (typical passport / visa digital photo)

  • Plain, light-coloured background — no objects or people behind you
  • Even lighting, no shadows or glare on your face or behind you
  • Face the camera straight on, neutral expression, mouth closed, eyes open
  • Head, shoulders and upper body visible; head fills ~50–70% of image height
  • No sunglasses or tinted glasses; no hat (unless religious/medical)
  • No filters or edits; recent photo (taken in the last month)
  • Output: JPEG, portrait 3:4-ish; this tool exports 1200 × 1500 px (well above the 600 × 750 minimum)

2 · Position yourself

Fit your face inside the green oval, eyes on the dashed line, shoulders inside the bottom marks. Use the 5-second timer so you can step back and relax.

Tip: stand ~1.5 m from a white/light wall, with a window or light in front of you (not behind).

Or use an existing photo

It will be centre-cropped to 4:5 and re-encoded as JPEG.

4 · Auto-checks

  • Resolution ≥ 600 × 750 px
  • Overall brightness OK (not too dark / not blown out)
  • Background looks light and plain (top corners sampled)
  • Lighting roughly even (left vs right)

These are heuristics to catch obvious problems — the official photo checker of whatever service you apply to has the final say. Face position, expression and glasses can't be auto-checked here.
